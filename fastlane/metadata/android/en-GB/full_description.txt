Kid Reels looks and swipes like the short-video reels apps children already love, but every reel is a tiny learning game.

Made for children who will not eat or sit still without reels: keep the swipe, swap the content for games that teach, and get a gentle, built-in end to screen time.

WHAT YOUR CHILD PLAYS
• Counting, adding and taking away, with matchsticks under the numbers
• Times tables shown as dot arrays, number patterns, which is more
• English ABC and Hindi वर्णमाला: "A for Apple", "अ से अनानास", and first-letter puzzles
• Shapes whose corners count themselves, 3D solids to spin with a finger
• Angles to drag open and see named, curves that draw themselves (ages 7 to 10)
• Telling the time, colours, big and small, odd one out, patterns
• One-square sudoku, with pictures for the youngest
• Animal sounds and animal homes

HOW IT KEEPS THINGS HEALTHY
• You set a daily time limit. Near the end only easy reels appear, then a calm goodbye reel says it is time to eat and play.
• An easy reel always follows a hard one; puzzles skipped come back a little easier.
• No streaks, points or badges to chase. A wrong tap is never called wrong: the voice says why it is not the answer and gives a hint.
• Settings open only after a grown-up holds a button for 3 seconds.
• Every few months, it asks you whether your child is ready for the next age.

FOR INDIAN FAMILIES
• Every line in up to three languages (English and Hindi now, more coming)
• Spoken aloud in the main language; Hindi letters in Hindi
• Ages 2 to 10, chosen by you

THREE LOOKS
Classic (dark, laid out like familiar reels apps), Candy (bright and bubbly) and Calm (soft and simple).

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No ads, no tracking, no account and no internet permission: nothing leaves the phone. Free and open source (MIT).

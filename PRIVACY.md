# Privacy

Kid Reels collects nothing.

- **No network access.** The app does not declare the `INTERNET` permission, so Android
  will not let it open a connection. Every reel is generated on the phone.
- **No accounts, ads, analytics, crash reporting or tracking** of any kind, and no
  third-party SDKs that could do these.
- **What is stored on the phone**, in the app's private storage: the parent's settings
  (languages, age, look, time limit, check-in interval, slow speech), today's minutes
  used, where each alphabet is up to, how often each topic was liked, and per-age counts
  of puzzles tried and answered first time (shown to the parent in settings and at the
  age check-in). The child's name and birthday are never asked for. Android's own backup
  may copy this file to your Google account or a new phone if you have backup turned on;
  the app itself sends it nowhere.
- **Speech:** words are handed to the text-to-speech engine installed on your phone (for
  example Google's Speech Services) to be spoken. That engine is a separate app with its
  own privacy policy; with the voice installed it works offline. "Install voice" only
  opens the engine's or the system's voice settings.
- **Permissions:** none.

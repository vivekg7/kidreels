Fun reels for children used to short videos: tap the tune, silly animals, bubble pop and copy me, where a drawn child acts out every move. Parents choose how many for each child, easing off by themselves over a few weeks. Meet Gappu, a baby elephant who yawns when time is nearly up and sleeps at bedtime. Every named word is now said in the child's other language too: "Apple means सेब". Settings can be in English or Hindi, apart from the reels' languages.
